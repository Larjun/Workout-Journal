## Shoes

### Nike Winflo 12 · `winflo-12` {#winflo-12}
- **Status:** active, main running shoe
- **Bought:** 2026-11-28
- **Why:** the most cushioned of the shoes I tried (Puma Darter Pro, adidas Adizero and Questar, Nike Pegasus 42, ASICS Gel Pulse 17). At my easy pace, protection matters more than weight or a responsive feel.

## Rules
