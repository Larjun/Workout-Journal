## Verdict
Legs, not lungs. HR stayed flat at ~155–161 through both reps (Zone 2 on the corrected max-HR zones), yet rep 2 was ~1 min/km slower and the legs faded. That's muscular endurance and impact tolerance running out, on the biggest running volume yet.

## What went wrong
- **First 500 m at ~9:46/km** (HR 142). The fast-start habit, again.
- **Volume jump on tired legs:** 25% more running than any previous session, two days after a hard sprint finish, with legs that were aching last week.
- **Humidity and short sleep** raised the cost of every km.

## Fix for next time
- First 500 m of each rep no faster than 12:30/km.
- Leg strength twice a week (calf raises, squats, split squats; 2–3 sets of 10–15).
- Before week 9: if legs still feel heavy, repeat this session rather than progressing.
