## Verdict
Zone 2 discipline held for the first ~2.3 km (avg 147). The finish wasn't an easy run: Zone 3, then an all-out sprint to 206 bpm instead of the cool-down.

## What went wrong
- **First km too quick again** (~10:58, then ~14:30): the yo-yo pattern from 11-18 in a new form.
- **Holding ≤154 forced a near-walking pace** (~13–14:30/km). That's a sign the Zone 2 ceiling may be set too low (see below), not just low fitness.
- **Sprinting replaced the cool-down.** Rain is a fair reason to get home, but it turned an easy session into a hard finish on legs that were aching last week.

## Finding: max HR is higher than Strava assumes
Strava's zones are calculated from a max HR of ~190 (Zone 2 = 125–154). Observed maxes: 199 (May 2026 ride), 192 (11-06 run), 206–207 today. Wet-wrist spikes are possible, but 199 is already confirmed. With max HR ≈ 200, Zone 2 would be ~130–162 and Zone 3 ~162–178, which matches the 160 easy-run cap already in use.

## Fix for next time
- **Update max HR in Strava to 200** (conservative) so zones and Relative Effort are realistic. Re-check if 205+ shows up again in dry conditions.
- Easy runs: cap at ~160 (new Zone 2 top), start the first km slower than you think.
- In rain, jog home at easy effort or walk; save sprints for planned sessions.
