# 2026-11-28 · Run · Continuous 4.25 km

**Plan:** Runna New To Running, week 8 of 9. 5 min walk, 4.25 km continuous at conversational pace, 5 min walk. Moved from Friday to give legs a rest day after 11-26.

## Verdict
Longest continuous run yet, and the best one. 4.25 km at ~11:48/km and avg HR 150, versus 3.75 km at ~12:39 and HR 154 on 11-18. Further, faster, at a lower heart rate. Aerobically comfortable, as it felt. The limiter is now clearly the feet and lower legs, not the engine.

## What went wrong
- **Km 2 surge** to ~10:49/km, then HR drifted up to 160+ for the last two km. The yo-yo habit, smaller this time.
- **Cadence still ~146**, only briefly at 148+. Landing under the hips while keeping the old stride length made 150 feel forced: the stride has to shorten for cadence to rise.
- **Feet struggled**, in shoes that are worn out and give little support.

## Fix for next time
- **Replace the running shoes before week 9's longer runs.** Done the same day: [Nike Winflo 12](../../gear/index.md#winflo-12).
- **Cadence:** keep the land-under-hips cue but take smaller, quieter steps, and drive the rhythm with a compact arm swing. Do 4–5 × 1 min at 150–152 inside easy runs, and set the metronome to 148 for the next two runs before moving to 150.
- Hold km 2 at the same pace as km 1; ease off whenever the watch shows under ~11:30/km.
- Rehydrate with ORS or salted buttermilk after humid runs; you lose more than a litre.
