## Verdict
HR goal essentially hit: 92% of the run under 160, peak 166, and the smallest drift of any continuous run so far (+12 bpm vs +25 on 11-17). Cadence goal missed: settled at ~146, not 150.

## What went wrong
- **Cadence plateaued at 146.** It barely moved from 11-17, and you only held 148+ for about a third of the run.
- **Yo-yo pacing.** The grade-adjusted pace graph swings between ~10:00 and ~14:00+/km every 0.5–0.8 km: surge, HR climbs, slow down or walk, repeat. A steady ~12:30 would have kept HR flatter with fewer walk breaks.
- **First km slightly quicker** than the rest (~11:53 vs ~13:10–13:47).
- Pace and walking were the cost of the HR cap. That's the right trade for now, but it shows how low the aerobic base still is.

## What went right
- Walked the cool-down home instead of running it.
- Back-to-back days and still a lower max HR than yesterday (166 vs 171, 180 with the run home).

## Fix for next time
- **Set the metronome to 152** to land at 148–150. Aiming at exactly 150 lands you at 146.
- Hold one steady effort instead of surging: when pace drops under ~11:30/km on the watch, ease off before HR forces a walk.
