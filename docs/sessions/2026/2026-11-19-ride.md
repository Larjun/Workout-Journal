## Verdict
Best aerobic ride yet: event speed (19.8 km/h on 11-15 at HR 162) at HR 137, almost entirely in Zone 2. Part of the gain is a stop-free route, but the HR gap is too big to be route alone.

## What went wrong
- **It was meant to be a rest day.** Run, run, then a 76-min ride: three days straight, and the legs are telling you.
- Slight drift: speed −3%, HR +4 bpm in the second half. Mild, but it's the fuel and fatigue starting to show.
- One spike to 163 on the Moopanar Bridge stretch; the flyover goal (under 155) held everywhere else.

## Fix for next time
- **Respect the rest day.** If you want to ride on it, 30 min at HR under 130, not 76 min.
- For rides over ~75 min, eat something at 45–60 min, even if the start felt fine.
- Friday run: if the soreness is a general muscle ache, run easy and shorter; if it's sharp or in one spot (calf, shin, Achilles), skip it.
