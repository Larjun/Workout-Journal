# 2026-11-20 · Swim · First swim test (pool)

**Plan:** First swim in ~2 years (last competitive swimming ~12 years ago), ahead of a pool super-sprint triathlon (300 m swim, 18 km bike, 3 km run). Breaststroke, 2 kicks per pull. Target: 100 m easy, 4 × 50 m, then 200–300 m continuous.

**Conditions:** 25 m pool. Arms (biceps, triceps) and front of both thighs sore afterwards.

*Breakdown is my reading of Apple Watch lap data, which is unreliable for breaststroke with frequent stops: treat counts and paces as approximate.*

## Verdict
The stroke is there; the swim fitness isn't yet. Roughly half the session was resting at the wall, and the data shows no continuous 200–300 m.

## What went wrong
- **Too much rest:** 1–3 min between single lengths. On race day 300 m means 12 lengths; at this pattern it would take 25+ min.
- **Heavy leg work:** 2-kick breaststroke loads the quads, which is why the front of the thighs is sore on top of Thursday's ride.

## Fix for next time
- Shorten rests, not effort: 6 × 50 m with **30 s** rest, then try 4 lengths (100 m) non-stop.
- Smaller, relaxed kicks with a longer glide to save the legs.
- Race-day target: 300 m with brief wall touches (~20–30 s max), about 13–15 min.
