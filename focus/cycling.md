## Rules
- Easy rides: HR 125–150, and you can talk the whole time.
- Shift down early on flyovers; spin, don't stand and push.
- Pulling away from signals: spin up in an easy gear.
- The day before a run: keep it 30–40 min and very easy.
- Ride-then-run back to back ("brick" workouts) not yet. Runs need fresh legs.
- Rides over 90 min: bring food (banana or dates). Red Bull isn't hydration; carry water.
- Front and rear lights plus something reflective for early rides.

## Recurring mistakes (from US riding history)
1. **Every ride at the same moderately hard effort.** Average HR 158–165 for hours (45 km at 158, 61 km at 162 for 3+ hours). Too hard to build base, too easy to count as real intensity.
2. **Speed plateaued for a year.** ~19–21 km/h in Oct 2025 and still ~19–20 in Sep 2026.
3. **Long gaps:** one ride in Feb, nothing mid-May to Aug, then only 3 rides before moving.
4. **Rode while sick** (St. Patrick's Day). Don't.
5. **Bike fit:** back hurt on the new road bike. Check fit and saddle height on the MTB.
