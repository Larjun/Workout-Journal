    Running focus¶

    Living document: update goals, mistakes and progress as they change. Last updated 2026-11-26.

    Context¶

    • Back to running after 3+ years off. Following the Runna "New To Running" plan (week 7 of 8 as of 2026-11-17).
    • Aerobically fitter than a true beginner (lifelong cyclist), but legs and calves aren't adapted to impact yet. Classic cyclist-turned-runner trap: the engine outruns the chassis.
    • Apple Watch VO2 max estimate ~31 (Nov 2026). Low for age; should rise quickly with consistent easy running.
    • HR zones (Strava): Z1 0–124 · Z2 125–154 · Z3 155–169 · Z4 170–184 · Z5 185+, based on an assumed max HR of ~190. Observed max is at least 199 (206–207 in a rain sprint on 11-24). With max ≈ 200: Z2 ~130–162, Z3 ~162–178. Update Strava's max HR to 200.

    Active goals¶

    Goal Target Status
    Cadence 148 spm for the whole run Stuck at ~146 (11-17, 11-18); only ~29% of 11-18 at 148+. Metronome now at 152
    Easy-run HR Under 160; under 165 late in the run ✅ 11-18: 92% under 160, peak 166, drift only +12 bpm
    Pacing Steady effort, no surges 11-18: yo-yo between ~10:00 and ~14:00/km every 0.5–0.8 km

    Rules for easy runs¶

    • Conversational pace means HR, not pace. Slow down or walk to stay under the cap.
    • Make the first rep or km the slowest.
    • At 2 km into continuous runs: if HR is above 158, shorten stride and drop 20–30 s/km.
    • If HR goes over 165: walk 20–30 s, then continue.
    • Short, quick steps landing under the hips (~60 cm steps at ~11:00–11:40/km).
    • Running home after the session: walk it, or jog at the same easy pace.
    • Drink before runs; stash a bottle on the loop on hot or humid days.

    Recurring mistakes¶

    1. Default pace is too fast. With no target, I settle at ~9:00–10:30/km and HR climbs into the 170s–180s (11-03, 11-04, 11-06, the 11-17 run home).
    2. Rep 1 faster than rep 2. Better since 11-11, but crept back on 11-13 (11:06 → 11:39).
    3. HR drift in the last km. Steady pace, but HR rises ~20–25 bpm (11-11, 11-17). Partly heat and humidity, partly fitness.
    4. Cadence lands below the target. 140 BPM dubstep pulled cadence to ~140; aiming at 150 lands at ~146 (11-18). Set the metronome a few beats above the goal (152).

    Current limiter¶

    • Legs, not heart. On 11-26 HR stayed flat (~155–161) while pace and legs faded in rep 2. Muscular endurance and impact tolerance lag the aerobic engine. Add leg strength twice a week and progress volume only when legs recover between runs.

    What's improving¶

    • Average HR on easy sessions: 164 (11-06) → 145 (11-11) → 142 (11-13) → 152 over a longer continuous 3 km (11-17).
    • Time in Z4+: ~21 min (11-06) → 5 s (11-11) → 0 (11-13).
    • Continuous running: 2 km at HR 162 / max 184 (11-03) → 3 km at HR 152 / max 171 (11-17) → 3.75 km at HR 154 / max 166 (11-18).
    • HR drift in continuous runs: +25 bpm (11-17) → +12 bpm (11-18), by capping HR from the start.
    • Cadence: ~138–140 → ~146 in the main effort (11-17).

    Notes¶

    • Calves got sore on 11-06. Doing calf raises (3 × 15, three times a week). Stop running for sharp or localized pain.
    • Whey protein after runs is fine; rehydrate separately with water and electrolytes.
